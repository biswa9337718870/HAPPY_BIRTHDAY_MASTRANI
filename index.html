Good Morning, Mora Geli!

Kebe bhabinathili ajana ru arambha heithiba eka kahani, dine mo jibana ra sabuthu sundara anubhaba palatijiba. College ra se lucha chhapa dekha, khelapadia re luchi luchi parasparaku dekhiva, picnic re unknown hei photo uthiba... se chhota chhota mulaakaat kebe ete bada smriti heigala, jana bi padilani. Se "Good Morning Bhai", "Good Afternoon Bhai" ra pachhare luchithiba hasa, Maggie, chocolate, tiffin time ra se daudiba, au daily 7 ru 9 parjyanta video call re biti jauthiba samaya — sabu aji bi mora mana re semiti sateja achhi.

Ajana thilu dine, aji ete nijara heigalu,

Chhota chhota dekha re kete swapna sajaigalu,

Samaya badalila, dina badalila, badalila ama parichaya,

Kintu tora pain mora bhalapaiba re rahigala sehi maya. ❤️

Puri ra se samudra, beach re ama masti, gote plate re khaiba, photo shoot ra se pagalapanti, au tu gadi chalauthiba bele tote dhariki basithiba se moment — sabu aji bi mora akhire nachijaye. Tora deithiba se shirt, Odissi dance bele tora se laja mishaa hasa, ama first hug au tora se chhota chhota smile... e sabu smriti ku samaya kebe puruna kariparibani.

Samudra ra lahari jemiti barambara kulaku chhuin jae,

Tora smriti semiti mo mana ku pratyeka dina chhuin jae,

Dunia pain e sabu hue ta kebala kichhi puruna katha,

Kintu mora pain e sabu mo jibana ra sabuthu sundara gatha. 🥹❤️

Aji 13th May ru 8th November — 179 days, mane 5 months and 26 days ama ei sundara journey ra. Ehi samaya bhitare kete hasa, kete abhimana, kete jhagara, kete manaiba au kete bhalapaiba jama heigala. Mora jibana ku asiba, mate bujhiba au mora bhalapaibaku accept kariba pain thank you, mora Geli.

Mu chahen nahi ama kahani kebala sundara dina gudikare simita rahu. Kebe rasta kathina hele ekathi chaliba, kebe mana kharap hele parasparaku bujhiba, au jete jhagara hele bi paraspara ra hata kebe chhadibani.

Khusi re tora hasa ra karana hebaku chahen,

Dukha re tora sabuthu bada sahara hebaku chahen,

Jibana ra pratyeka modare tora sathi hei rahibi,

Ehi janma nuhe, pratyeka janmare tote hi bhalapaibi. ❤️

Mora Dipali, mora Dipina, mora Mastrani, mora Geli, mora Bulbul... November mora pain kebala eka month nuhe, eha sehi manisha ra janma month, jie mora sadharana dina gudiku bi special karideichhi. Bhagaban nka pakhare mora etiki prarthana — tora sabu swapna pura heu, tora jibana khusire bhari rahu, au tora muhare sehi hasa sabubele rahu.

Birthday ta barsare thare asiba,

Kintu tora pain mora bhalapaiba pratyeka dina badhiba,

Aji tora janma dina ku celebrate karibaku mu ete excited,

Kintu tora saha sara jibana ra pratyeka birthday manaiba mora sabuthu bada khusi. ❤️

I LOVE YOU SO MUCH, MY DIPALI! ❤️🥹

Tora hasa mora khusi, tora khusi mora prarthana, au tora sath mora jibana ra sabuthu sundara upahara. Ehi bhalapaiba emiti hi rahu, ama kahani re au hajara hajara nua smriti jodi heu.

Forever Yours,

Mora Geli, Mora Dipali! ❤️🫂😘
